fastmaps privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026.

fastmaps is a car navigation app. It has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. What it knows about you stays on your phone, except for what it must send to the map and traffic services below to do its job.

What leaves your phone, and why

  • Your location and destinations go to TomTom and Google (Routes and Places) to plan routes, read live traffic and find places, and to OpenStreetMap's Overpass servers to load speed limits, cameras, road signs and parking near you.
  • What you type in search goes to TomTom and Google Places to find matching places.
  • Spoken directions: the text of an instruction, such as a street name, goes to Google Cloud Text-to-Speech to be turned into speech.
  • nap.si (optional): if you sign in with your own nap.si account, your nap.si email address and password go only to nap.si to sign you in; the app then keeps nap.si's sign-in token on your phone.
  • Spotify (optional): if you connect Spotify, you sign in on Spotify's own page; the app keeps Spotify's token on your phone and asks Spotify for your playlists and what is playing. The song title and artist go to lrclib.net to fetch lyrics.
  • Police enforcement spots are downloaded from policija.si; nothing about you is sent.

All of this is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each service handles the data under its own privacy policy. fastmaps does not sell, share or keep any of it.

What stays on your phone

Saved places (home, work, favourites), recent searches, your settings, speed traps you report, where you tend to get stuck in traffic, and the nap.si and Spotify sign-in tokens. Calls: to show who is calling and let you answer or hang up from the map, the app reads the caller's number and looks the name up in your contacts on the phone. Call and contact details are never sent anywhere.

Deleting your data

There is nothing stored about you outside your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it kept. Signing out of nap.si in Settings deletes the nap.si token.

Children

fastmaps is meant for drivers and is not directed at children.

Pravilnik o zasebnosti za fastmaps

Zadnja sprememba 22. novembra 2026.

fastmaps je navigacija za avto. Nima svojih računov, oglasov, analitike ali svojega strežnika. Kar ve o vas, ostane na vašem telefonu, razen tega, kar mora poslati spodnjim storitvam za zemljevide in promet, da lahko deluje.

Kaj zapusti telefon in zakaj

  • Vaša lokacija in cilji gredo k TomTomu in Googlu (Routes in Places) za načrtovanje poti, promet v živo in iskanje krajev ter na strežnike Overpass za OpenStreetMap za omejitve hitrosti, radarje, prometne znake in parkirišča v bližini.
  • Kar vpišete v iskanje, gre k TomTomu in Google Places za iskanje krajev.
  • Glasovna navodila: besedilo navodila, na primer ime ulice, gre v Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, ki ga pretvori v govor.
  • nap.si (izbirno): če se prijavite s svojim računom nap.si, gresta vaš e-poštni naslov in geslo za nap.si samo k nap.si za prijavo; aplikacija nato na telefonu hrani žeton za prijavo.
  • Spotify (izbirno): če povežete Spotify, se prijavite na Spotifyjevi strani; aplikacija na telefonu hrani Spotifyjev žeton in od Spotifyja dobi vaše sezname predvajanja in trenutno pesem. Naslov pesmi in izvajalec gresta na lrclib.net za besedilo.
  • Točke policijskih kontrol se prenesejo s policija.si; o vas se ne pošlje nič.

Vse to se pošilja po šifrirani povezavi (HTTPS). Vsaka storitev podatke obdela po svojem pravilniku o zasebnosti. fastmaps jih ne prodaja, ne deli in ne hrani.

Kaj ostane na telefonu

Shranjeni kraji (dom, služba, priljubljeni), nedavna iskanja, nastavitve, prijavljene kontrole hitrosti, mesta, kjer navadno obtičite v prometu, ter žetona za nap.si in Spotify. Klici: da vidite, kdo kliče, in klic sprejmete ali končate z zemljevida, aplikacija prebere številko klicatelja in ime poišče med stiki na telefonu. Podatki o klicih in stikih se nikamor ne pošiljajo.

Izbris podatkov

Zunaj vašega telefona ni shranjeno nič o vas. Z odstranitvijo aplikacije ali brisanjem njene shrambe v nastavitvah Androida izbrišete vse, kar je hranila. Z odjavo iz nap.si v nastavitvah izbrišete žeton za nap.si.

Otroci

fastmaps je namenjen voznikom in ni namenjen otrokom.
